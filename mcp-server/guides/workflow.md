# Vox explainer workflow (coded edition)

You are producing a narrated Vox-style motion-graphics explainer. Every frame is
coded: Remotion templates render the scenes, ElevenLabs voices the narration,
and nothing is generated by a video model. Your job is the editorial work —
research, script, storyboard — and driving the tools in order.

## Pipeline

| Step | What you do | Tool | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Brief | Ask the questions below, one at a time, each with a recommended default | — | free |
| 2 Research | Find the story, verify every number against two independent sources | your own web search | free |
| 3 Script | Write N narration blocks | — | free |
| 4 Storyboard | Pick one scene template per block and fill its fields | `vox_list_scene_types` | free |
| 5 Save | Validate and store the project; fix every warning | `vox_save_project` | free |
| 6 Preview | Render a still of each block and look at it | `vox_render_preview` | free |
| **7 Gate** | **Show script, storyboard, previews, and the estimate. Stop and wait for a yes.** | `vox_plan_timing` | — |
| 8 Voice | Synthesize narration with word timestamps | `vox_synthesize_narration` | ElevenLabs characters |
| 9 Music (optional) | Instrumental bed, ducked under the voice automatically | `vox_generate_music` | ElevenLabs music credits |
| 10 Render | Export the MP4, then report the path | `vox_render_video`, `vox_render_status` | local CPU |

**Exactly one approval gate, at step 7.** Nothing before it spends ElevenLabs
credits. Previews before the gate are silent and timed from word counts; that
is expected. After narration exists, re-run `vox_plan_timing` — block lengths
now come from the real audio.

## 1. Brief

Ask one question per turn, first option recommended. If the user already
answered something in their request, pre-select it rather than skipping it.

1. **Topic** — what they typed, or "find me something trending".
2. **Length** — 1 minute (~6 blocks, recommended) · 30 seconds (3) · 3 minutes (18).
3. **Aspect** — 9:16 vertical (recommended) · 16:9 · 1:1. Rendering the other
   aspects later is free; layouts adapt.
4. **Voice** — auto-pick a deep, measured documentary narrator from
   `vox_list_voices` (recommended) · let me pick · use voice id X.
5. **Music** — none (recommended) · generate an instrumental bed · I'll supply a file.

Ask one extra clarifier only when guessing wrong would waste the run.

## 2. Research

- An article URL: read it, extract the claim, the numbers and the angle, then
  find two more sources so the script is not one outlet's framing.
- A trending term: the term is a symptom; find the specific story behind it
  with a number in it.
- No topic: search what is moving this week in AI, tech or money and pick a
  story with a why/how question, one surprising number, and a reversal.

Every number needs two independent sources. No invented quotes, no invented
numbers. A vague true line beats a specific false one. Record sources in the
project's `sources` array. Avoid breaking tragedies and celebrity gossip.

## 3. Script

N blocks, one idea each, **~20–24 words** (about 8–9 seconds spoken).

- Plain spoken text. Spell numbers out ("seventy percent", "twenty twenty-four").
- No em dashes, no stage directions, never "in this video".
- Commas for pace, periods only for beats you want to land.
- Tone: Vox house documentary. Curious, precise, a little wry. The narrator
  explains and never hypes. No direct address, no hype adjectives.

Shape:

- **Block 1, cold open** — the most surprising fact or question, stated flat.
- **Block 2, stakes** — why it is weird, or why it matters to the viewer.
- **Middle, evidence** — one number, date, place or comparison per block. Escalate.
- **Block N−1, the turn** — the counterintuitive reveal.
- **Block N, resolution** — a kicker line that reframes block 1.

## 4. Storyboard

Call `vox_list_scene_types` for the exact fields. Rules that make it read like
a film rather than a slideshow:

- **The scene shows the number the narration says, while it says it.** A stat
  card under the wrong sentence is a factual misread.
- **Vary the rhythm.** Never two of the same scene type in a row. Alternate
  big type (headline, stat) with structure (bar_chart, line_chart, timeline,
  comparison, list).
- **One question hook.** Put the question in a block 1 `headline`; answer it in
  the `end_card`.
- **Through-line.** Reuse one word, color or figure across scenes (the same
  highlight word, the same company bar painted red) so the viewer tracks it.
- **Keep on-screen text short.** The narration carries the sentence; the screen
  carries the number and the noun. Headlines under ~8 words.
- `quote` is for verified, verbatim quotes only.
- `image` needs an asset: add it with `vox_add_asset` from a URL or path you
  have the rights to use (press kits, public-domain maps, your own files).
  Halftone treatment by default. Do not use photos of real people to imply
  things they did not say or do.
- End on `end_card` with the kicker and the source names.

## 5–6. Save and preview

`vox_save_project` validates everything and returns warnings. Treat each
warning as a defect and fix it before the gate. Then `vox_render_preview` each
block (`at: 0.7` shows the scene after its animations land) and actually look
at the image: overflowing text, collisions with the caption band, and empty
frames are only visible there.

## 7. The gate

Show, in one message:

- Topic and angle, plus one runner-up.
- The script, block by block, with each block's scene type.
- The preview stills, or their paths.
- `vox_plan_timing` output: estimated runtime and ElevenLabs characters.
- Sources.

Then stop. Do not synthesize until the user says yes.

## 8–10. Build

1. `vox_synthesize_narration` (all blocks). It skips blocks whose text and voice
   are unchanged, so re-running after an edit only pays for what changed.
2. If a block runs past ~9.5 seconds, shorten its line, save, and re-synthesize
   that block.
3. Optional `vox_generate_music`, then set `music` in the project and save.
4. `vox_render_preview` one or two blocks again to check captions against the
   real timing.
5. `vox_render_video`. If it returns `rendering`, poll `vox_render_status` until
   `done`. Report the MP4 path, duration and the source list.

Assemble in the same run. Loose audio files are not a deliverable.
